About the App

Your Growth Window Is the Most Important Window

The Science

Built on Peer-Reviewed Research

Everything in GetTaller maps to established medical models — no pills, no magic, no false promises.

Khamis-Roche (1994)

Pediatric height-prediction method combining parental heights with the child's current height & weight.

Tanner Mid-Parental Method

The standard genetic baseline: (Father + Mother ± 13) ÷ 2 — used to set each user's realistic potential.

HGH & Deep Sleep — Van Cauter (2000)

~75% of daily growth hormone is released during slow-wave sleep, concentrated in the first hours of sleep.

Growth Plate Biology

Longitudinal growth stops at ~18 (F) / ~21 (M). The app segments users around this biological reality.

Spinal Decompression

Gravity-based training (hanging, swimming) temporarily increases disc height and improves spinal alignment.

Dietary Reference Intakes

Calcium & protein targets follow official IOM guidelines for peak bone-mineral accrual in adolescence.
